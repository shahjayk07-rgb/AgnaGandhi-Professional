About Agna

Corporate work, understood in business context.

Agna Gandhi is an Associate at Crawford Bayley & Co. Advocates & Solicitors. Her stated focus is general corporate and commercial matters, outside litigation.

Experience shaped by practical legal work

Corporate and commercial matters often require careful reading, structured research and attention to how a document will operate in practice. Work may involve understanding a business arrangement, identifying the legal questions it raises, reviewing the relevant documents and helping organise the issues for consideration.

Agna’s professional focus is general corporate work. The precise nature of any matter, scope of work and availability would need to be assessed individually.

Education and professional development

Agna’s public profile identifies D. M. Harish School of Law, where her listed education period is 2022–2026. A school prospectus also records her participation in the 2nd Edition National Moot Court Competition organised by D. Y. Patil University, Navi Mumbai, on 30–31 March 2024.

Her academic and professional development includes legal research, written analysis and working through legal issues in their commercial context. Her academic interests also include Company Secretary (CS) studies, alongside legal research and corporate and commercial law.

Areas of interest

General corporate matters, commercial agreements, corporate documentation, company-related legal research and compliance questions that arise in the course of business.
