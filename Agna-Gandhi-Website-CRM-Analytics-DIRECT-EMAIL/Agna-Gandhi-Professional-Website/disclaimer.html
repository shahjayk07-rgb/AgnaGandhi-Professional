How to understand and use the information published on this website.

General information, not legal advice

The content on this website is provided for general informational and educational purposes. It is not intended to be, and should not be relied on as, legal advice or a substitute for advice based on the facts and documents of a particular matter.

No engagement through website access

Visiting this website, following a link or sending an initial enquiry does not by itself create a lawyer–client relationship or confirm that any matter will be accepted. Any engagement would require appropriate checks, confirmation of availability, an agreed scope and any applicable formalities.

Do not send confidential information

Please do not send confidential, privileged, commercially sensitive or time-critical material through an initial enquiry. A conflict check and an appropriate arrangement should be completed before sharing sensitive documents or detailed facts.

Accuracy and external links

Reasonable care is taken when preparing website content, but laws and circumstances can change and no representation is made that every page is complete, current or suitable for every situation. External links are provided for convenience; their content and availability are outside this website’s control.

Professional and regulatory context

Nothing on this website is intended to solicit work in a manner prohibited by applicable professional rules. Descriptions of subject areas are general in nature and should not be read as a guarantee of availability, outcome or acceptance of any matter.
