Insight

Before signing a commercial agreement.

A practical starting checklist for reading a business contract.

Read beyond the headline terms

Identify the parties, scope, payment terms, key dates, responsibilities, confidentiality obligations, termination provisions and the process for handling changes.

Questions to ask

  • Are the parties and details correct?
  • Are deliverables, timelines and acceptance criteria clear?
  • Are fees, expenses and payment dates specified?
  • Who owns or may use intellectual property?
  • What happens if performance is delayed or the relationship ends?
  • Are notice and governing-law clauses understood?

This checklist is educational, not a substitute for reviewing the specific agreement.
